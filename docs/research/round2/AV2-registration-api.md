## 1. Summary

- **The spec is not login-gated.** The audit says the specification "remains login-gated and unread". That is wrong. The full OpenAPI 3.1 bundle is public and has been read. Most of section 3.11's "third party" and "still unknown" items can now be settled from the official text.
- **Webhooks: REFUTED as "user events only".** The API has a full `/webhooks` resource with 6 topics: Media, Playlist, Layout, Schedule, Screen and User. Each topic carries create, update and delete events. Payloads are signed with HMAC-SHA256 (`x-signage-signature`). Registration goes through a 3-request signature validation. Endpoints must be HTTPS on port 443 and answer within 5 s, and are re-validated every 90 days. The webhooks have **no online/offline, push-completed or media-processed topics**, so the "documented gap" in the audit should be narrowed rather than dropped.
- **Rate limits are now official and numeric.** Low is 14 per 10 s per token and 42 per account. Standard is 21 and 63. High is 33 and 99. 106 of the 110 operations are `standard`, 2 are `high` (emergency-alert reads), 2 are unlabelled and **none are `low`**. Limits apply per token and per account; no plan dimension is stated.
- **Push status is not job-scoped.** `POST /screens/push` returns a fixed `push_status_url` (`.../screens/push/status`) with no job ID. The status object is `initialized | in_progress {index, of, percent} | completed {screens[id,name]} | failed {errors}`. The audit's "job-scoped status and per-screen results" describes what TINNOX should build. It is not what Yodeck provides.
- **The per-type registration prefix table exists, and the audit's partial table was wrong.** The prefixes are 0 Yodeck Player (Pi), 1 Web Player, 2 BrightSign, 3 Android, 4 Tizen, 5 webOS, 6 Windows Intel-only or W Windows standard, 7 Fire OS, 8 Android (Play Store), 9 ChromeOS and 10 Embeddable Feeds (H/654056; SPEC agrees for 0 to 9). The audit's "8 Fire OS" is REFUTED: 8 is Android Play Store and Fire OS is 7. The registration code can also be supplied through `POST /screens` (`reg_code`), which SPEC says must be "exactly 9 digits".
- **Time zone in the API is two strings: `basic.time_zone` (region) and `basic.time_subzone` (city).** These are not IANA IDs. The default is `Etc`/`UTC`. All Australian zones appear, including `Eucla`, `Lord_Howe`, `Broken_Hill`, `Perth` and `Darwin`, as well as legacy aliases (`ACT`, `NSW`, `North`, `South`, `West`, `Queensland`, `Victoria`, `Tasmania`, `LHI`, `Yancowinna`, `Canberra`, `Currie`, `Lindeman`). The UTC labels in the tables are inconsistent snapshots: Melbourne shows "UTC+11" (summer time) while London shows "UTC+0" (standard time). Neither SPEC nor any of the 656 articles mentions daylight saving (0 hits).
- **Availability datetimes are naive local times.** SPEC says `available_after` and `available_before` contain no time zone and are "interpreted based on the screen's location". The examples nonetheless carry a `Z` suffix, which contradicts that. Schedule event `start` and `end` are naive (`2024-01-01T00:00:00`). Server timestamps are UTC ISO 8601 with `Z`.
- **Android players cannot take a time zone or NTP setting from Yodeck at all** (H/455948, 30 Sep 2026). They also cannot take network or resolution settings, and CEC is unsupported. This matters a great deal for TINNOX, whose Huidu-class controllers are Android. The audit's screen-properties row implies the time zone works on every player type.
- **Factory pre-provisioning is now an official, productised flow** (H/887679, updated today). Ordering players auto-creates one screen per player. The buyer then has a 5-hour window to name the screens, set Wi-Fi, 3G/4G/LTE, proxy or static IP, and choose workspaces. Each player is "bound to its screen before it ships".
- **Plans (4.3): mostly confirmed, with four corrections.** (a) The free tier is now two-stage: 30 days, 5 screens and all features for sign-ups from 2 March 2026, then one free Basic screen. (b) "Same rate monthly or annual" is not supported. Help pages say the listed prices are annual and monthly "may incur slightly higher rates". (c) Premium and Enterprise rose by USD 1 from 1 April 2026. (d) Enterprise pages say Enterprise is "free if you only manage one screen", which conflicts with the "one free Basic screen" rule.
- **Tally: 64 claims checked (rows 1–64). 38 confirmed, 17 corrected, 7 refuted, 2 still unknown (rows 24 and 53). Further open points are listed in section 4.**

### 3.2 Schemas TINNOX should note

- **Screen object** (SPEC `ScreenFullDetail`): `id`, `uuid`, `name`, `description`, `player_type` (free string, e.g. "Raspberry", "Web Player"), `workspace`, `created_at`, `last_modified`, `last_ip_address`, `last_pushed`, `reg_code`, `takeover_content`, `screenshot_url`, `state {online, last_seen, updating, registered}`, `configuration`, `gpio_enable`, plus the nine opt-in subsets. Provisioning by API is possible: `POST /screens` accepts `name`, `player_type`, `workspace`, `reg_code` and `working_hours_config`.
- **Offline alerting**: `offline_notifications {notify_after (minutes), notify_during_scheduled_turnoff, notify_when_back_online, recipients, recipients_all}`. The UI lets the delay range from 30 minutes to 48 hours in 30-minute steps (H/456051). This is e-mail only, with no webhook.
- **Webhook security model**: an HMAC key shown once; validation challenge (2 bad and 1 good signatures, in random order; the endpoint must answer 401 or 2xx); HTTPS on port 443; 5 s timeout; no cookies; re-validation every 90 days; `retry_count` and `last_failure_reason` exposed. This is a good template for TINNOX's outbound webhooks (PROPOSED).
- **Push status** (§2 row 46): one account-level status, `index/of/percent` progress, completed screen list. Its weakness is that concurrent pushes cannot be told apart (INFERRED from the lack of a job ID). TINNOX should return a job ID (PROPOSED).

### 3.3 Hosting and data-sovereignty signals (relevant to TINNOX Q9)

- Media uploads go to S3 `dsbackend` in **`eu-west-1`** (Ireland). Playback-report CSVs come from `signage-playback-reports` in **`us-east-1`** (SPEC example pre-signed URLs). Both are VERIFIED_FULL_PAGE as examples only; whether all customer data lives there is INFERRED. Yodeck therefore shows no Australian data residency. That supports TINNOX choosing AU-region hosting (ap-southeast-2 or equivalent) as a differentiator (PROPOSED).

### 3.4 LED, controllers, Android (Huidu-class) players

- **LED**: H/455837 (this week) still documents only the Pi path. Force the "DVI resolution", note that LED panels "just use the top-left corner of the input", and disable the right and bottom pixels using overscan parameters (e.g. 1920×1080 input to a 320×240 panel). Overscan reboots the Pi. API `sound_and_display.overscan` is a string such as `"4,3,5,1"`. **NovaStar, Huidu, Colorlight, Linsn and "sending card" brands: 0 mentions in 656 articles.** H/456149 claims "hundreds of production-grade LED billboards" (marketing, 31 Oct 2025).
- **Android limitations** (H/455948, 30 Sep 2026): no CEC (black screen instead); no custom certificates, SSH or storage encryption; no sound-output selection; no USB capture; web pages always render on top in layouts; and, "temporarily unavailable", **no network, resolution, time zone or NTP management**. Huidu and most Chinese LED players are Android-based (HARDWARE-DEPENDENT/UNVERIFIED for any given model). On those, Yodeck could not set the time zone remotely at all. TINNOX's agent must own time zone and NTP on Android (PROPOSED, MVP).
- **Kiosk**: the Android Basic tab offers "Kiosk Mode" to lock touch devices to signage, plus TouchScreen enablement (H/455844). The API's `advanced.interactive_features` covers touch and virtual keyboard settings. 40 articles mention kiosk.
- **Cellular**: 3G/4G/LTE modems are configurable on the Pi (`network.modem`: APN, PIN, credentials, advanced config) and in the order-time setup. This is relevant for roadside pylons.
- **Video walls**: H/601510 (10 Mar 2026) says plainly that "The screens on the Video Wall are not 100% synced", and NTP is pitched for "frame-accurate" sync (H/482103). This is a gap TINNOX can avoid claiming.

### 3.5 Australia

- AUD is a supported billing currency (H/456067). Support hours list "Sydney (Australian Eastern)" (H/178348). The Date & Time app code lists 23 `Australia/*` IANA zones (H/456347), while the screen setting uses the zone/subzone split.
- The legacy aliases (`ACT`, `NSW`, `North`, `South`, `West`, `LHI`, `Yancowinna`, `Canberra`, `Currie`) are offered alongside the canonical zones. That invites inconsistent picks for the same city. TINNOX should offer only the canonical IANA IDs (Australia/Perth, Eucla, Darwin, Adelaide, Broken_Hill, Brisbane, Lindeman, Sydney, Melbourne, Hobart, Lord_Howe) (PROPOSED).

### 3.6 Monitoring

- Status subset fields: CPU temperature, CPU load, swap, HDMI connected, power supply, Wi-Fi status, uptime, storage, RAM, software and hardware version, firmware, lockdown and encryption (most are Pi-only). A useful baseline for the TINNOX device telemetry schema (PROPOSED).
- Portal content states: Up to date, Downloading, Unknown, Pending Media, Possibly Outdated, Not Updated (H/456254).
