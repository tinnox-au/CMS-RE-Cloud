# AV1: adversarial verification of audit sections 3.1 and 3.10 (accounts, roles, workspaces, partner console, plans, support, compliance)

Date: 2026-11-03. Target: `docs/yodeck-workflow-audit.md` sections 3.1 and 3.10, plus rows of 4.3, 4.4 and contradiction C3/C4/C11/C12 that repeat the same claims. Repository files were not edited.

Sources: full text of the Yodeck help centre (656 articles, local copy at `scratchpad/web/art/`) and the public OpenAPI spec (`scratchpad/web/probe-api-docs_bundle.yaml`, fetched 2026-11-03 from https://app.yodeck.com/api-docs/bundle.yaml). HELP = `https://help.yodeck.com/en/articles/`. "UPDATED: this week" means the week ending 2026-11-03. No WebSearch calls were used.

## Summary

- 46 claims checked. **30 confirmed**, **12 corrected**, **1 refuted**, **3 still unknown** (rows 31, 41, 43). Rows 26 and 46 are partly unknown: the audit-log file formats and the partner discount percentages. 43 rows now have at least part of the claim VERIFIED_FULL_PAGE.
- **Refuted: what happens when a workspace is deleted.** The audit says items move to a hidden Default Workspace. The current page says the workspace's content is "permanently deleted". Only its screens are moved out.
- **Corrections that change decisions:**
  - **Support hours.** Every plan, Free included, gets 24/5 live chat and email. This is not an Enterprise extra. The "within 10 hours" first-response figure and the Enterprise 24/5 and Enterprise Plus 24/7 tiers do not appear in the help centre. Seen from Australia, live support opens Monday 1:00 pm AEST or 3:00 pm AEDT. The Australian phone line runs roughly 5:00 pm to 8:00 am AEST, which is mostly outside Australian business hours.
  - **Monthly versus annual price.** The audit says the per-screen rate is the same either way. Two current pages say the published rate assumes annual billing and that monthly "may incur slightly higher rates".
  - **Audit log columns.** The customer audit log has seven columns: Time, Session, Workspace, User, Section, Name, Action. "Object", "Change Message" and "Support user" belong to the partner console log, not the customer log.
  - **Partner terms are now first-party.** The 10-screen minimum is applied across the whole partner site. Fees are USD 50 one-off (Reseller) and USD 450 a year (Whitelabel). Downgrades take effect at the next renewal. Partner sites are suspended at 60 days overdue and their screens disabled at 90 days. None of this is THIRD PARTY any more.
  - **Partner staff permissions.** There are eight permissions, not five. Update Accounts, Update Billing-related, Support Login and Documentation are on by default when someone is promoted to staff.
  - **Billing Entities.** Enterprise and Enterprise Plus both include it (annual, invoiced subscription only). Basic and Premium can buy a limited paid add-on.
  - **The trial runs on the Enterprise plan.** The account is paused at day 30 for exactly that reason.
- **Resolved unknowns:**
  - Invitations can be resent.
  - Screen Content has no Delete permission (help article and spec agree).
  - The API rate-limit numbers are now first-party: Low 14/42, Standard 21/63, High 33/99 per 10 seconds. This closes C3.
  - AWS Ireland (eu-west-1) hosting is stated on a help page, and the API's S3 upload URLs carry eu-west-1 as well.
  - Lock-down and storage encryption are both Raspberry Pi only.
- **Still unknown:** ISO 27001 and 9001, SOC 2, the DPA, the sub-processor list and the Swedish location. These claims came from www.yodeck.com or the trust pages, which are blocked here; no help article states them. The same goes for the 365-day deletion of inactive accounts, the 50 percent partner discount, and whether the customer can see partner support logins in its own audit log.
