## Summary

- I checked **87 claims**: 43 CONFIRMED (several upgraded from THIRD PARTY or snippet level to VERIFIED_FULL_PAGE), 27 CORRECTED, 12 REFUTED, 5 STILL UNKNOWN.
- **Biggest corrections** (each changes TINNOX design text or the priorities):
  1. **Webhooks exist.** The API has HMAC-signed webhooks with a Screen topic. The audit lists webhooks as a gap.
  2. **Per-screen notification recipients exist.** Only users with Device Manager access can be chosen. The audit says this is an open feedback request.
  3. **The Status tab field list is fully documented**, and it is exposed in the API through `subset=player_status`. Fields: CPU temperature, CPU load, RAM, swap, uptime, software version, Wi-Fi, HDMI and power supply. The audit says UNKNOWN.
  4. **The update cadence is documented.** Regular updates ship every 6 weeks on Tuesdays. Emergency hotfixes come 1 to 2 times a year. "Updating" for more than 1 to 2 hours means the update failed and needs remote manual intervention. The audit says UNKNOWN.
  5. **Cached playback starts without the network.** The Pi shows "Running startup diagnostics", then plays from the local cache after 2 minutes if it holds a valid schedule file and media. The audit says UNKNOWN.
  6. **There are six content states, not five.** "Pending Media" is shown when Restricted Download Hours are on.
  7. **"35 days" is official.** Article 456183 says "at least 30-35 days". The audit dropped it as third-party only.
  8. **Scheduled reboot is official.** It appears in the Timed Events article and as `timed_events.schedule_reboot` in the API. The audit says forum only.
  9. **The refresh-screenshot and Playback Reports API endpoints are official** (both in the spec). The audit has them as THIRD PARTY and "shipping unconfirmed".
  10. **The playback CSV columns are documented.**
  11. **The SETTINGS.txt `[PROXY]` keys are documented.**
  12. **S3 and packages hosts are needed by every player**, not only Windows.
  13. **"Capturing screenshot..." means a capture is running now.** The message for a capture queued after reconnect is "Your screenshot will appear here soon".
- **One quoted string is not supported:** "Content not updated" (offline at push time) appears in none of the 656 articles.
- **The RTC and hourly time-save claims are not in the help centre.** They probably came from a www.yodeck.com/docs page, which is blocked. They stay snippet-level.
