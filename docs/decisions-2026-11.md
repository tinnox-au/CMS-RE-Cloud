# TINNOX CMS decisions, November 2026

Date: 2026-11-03. Status: for TINNOX approval. This page records, in one place, TINNOX's answers to Q1 to Q12 and the round-2 research decisions that change the design. The long documents carry the detail. This page is what TINNOX approves or amends.

How to read the table:
- **Source** names the TINNOX answer (Qn, see `open-questions.md` section 2) or the round-2 note under `research/round2/`. Contradiction IDs C1 to C15 refer to `research/round2/R99-critic.md` section 1.
- **Status** is either **DECIDED by client** (TINNOX has said so) or **PROPOSED for TINNOX approval** (our recommendation). Where a row mixes both, the cell says which part is which.
- **Affected docs** uses short names: tinnox-context, mvp-spec (`tinnox-mvp-spec.md`), architecture (`architecture-and-hardware.md`), backlog (`implementation-backlog.md`), acceptance-tests, open-questions, yodeck-audit (`yodeck-workflow-audit.md`), rea-evaluation.
- Evidence labels follow the rest of the pack: VERIFIED_FULL_PAGE, OFFICIALLY DOCUMENTED, THIRD PARTY, INFERRED, UNKNOWN, HARDWARE-DEPENDENT/UNVERIFIED, PROPOSED. The authenticated Yodeck interface was not inspected. Yodeck is a reference only, and nothing is copied.
- Open questions to TINNOX use the new IDs Q13 to Q26 in `open-questions.md` section 2.1. Each one maps to Q-T1 to Q-T14 in R99 section 3.

## 2. Recommended answers to Q8, Q9 and Q10

### 2.1 Q8: how vendors give support staff access to customer accounts

TINNOX asked how normal CMS vendors, for example Samsung, do this. There is no single industry norm (R2). Signage vendors fall into three groups: default access, customer-granted access with an expiry, and customer approval per request.

| Vendor | How support reaches customer accounts | Customer control | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Samsung MagicINFO Server (on-premise) | No standing access. The customer runs the server. | Full: it is the customer's own infrastructure | THIRD PARTY |
| Samsung MagicINFO Cloud and VXT | Samsung hosts the data. Its business privacy notice says support staff "may sometimes have incidental access" to customer data. No customer grant or approval control was found. | The customer Owner invites named partner users into a workspace with a role and can withdraw them. No expiry found. | OFFICIALLY DOCUMENTED (search summary); expiry and audit UNKNOWN |
| LG ConnectedCare and SuperSign Cloud | System integrators manage customer displays remotely. | User privileges, access control and SSO. No public detail on consent or audit. | OFFICIALLY DOCUMENTED (marketing); consent UNKNOWN |
| Xibo Cloud | Standing on-demand access: "We may access your Xibo CMS on demand using an administrator account in order to provide support" (terms 3.5). | Need-to-know limit in the DPA. Inspection rights on 5 working days' notice. No expiry. | VERIFIED_FULL_PAGE |
| Yodeck (partner console) | Partner staff with "Support Login" can log in as any customer user. The permission is on by default for new staff. | No customer-side switch documented. Partner audit log records each login. The customer audit log is Enterprise-only and keeps 90 days. | VERIFIED_FULL_PAGE (help centre) |
| Signagelive (by Navori) | No access without a grant. | Three modes: approve a Network Access Request; pre-authorise a named team to self-grant 24 h at a time; or grant indefinite access, revocable at any time. | OFFICIALLY DOCUMENTED (search summary) |
| **TINNOX B+ (PROPOSED)** | Device operations always on under contract. Content and account access only with a customer grant (24 h default, 7 days maximum), optional standing access for Managed customers, optional 24 h self-grant, two-person break-glass. | Customer admins grant, see and revoke every session in their own audit log. | PROPOSED (DEC-17) |
